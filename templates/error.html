About GreenGuard

Our Mission

GreenGuard aims to provide farmers and agricultural professionals with data-driven crop recommendations to optimize yields and promote sustainable farming practices.

How It Works

Our system uses machine learning algorithms to analyze soil nutrients, pH levels, and environmental factors to recommend the most suitable crops for your specific conditions.

The Technology

The crop recommendation system is powered by a Random Forest model trained on a dataset of 2,200 soil samples and their corresponding successful crops. Our model achieves an accuracy rate of over 95% in predicting optimal crop selection.
